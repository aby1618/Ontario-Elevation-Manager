# Data Sources and Attribution

Ontario Elevation Manager accesses public elevation datasets published by the Government of Ontario.

## Ontario Digital Terrain Model (LiDAR-Derived)

The Ontario DTM is a raster representation of bare-earth terrain derived from classified LiDAR point clouds.

Ontario describes the dataset as non-overlapping 1 km by 1 km tiles grouped into larger downloadable packages.

Official catalogue:
https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/ontario-digital-terrain-model-lidar-derived

Official GeoHub page:
https://geohub.lio.gov.on.ca/maps/mnrf::ontario-digital-terrain-model-lidar-derived/about

## Ontario Digital Surface Model (LiDAR-Derived)

The Ontario DSM represents the highest reflective surface and may include buildings, vegetation, towers, poles, and other surface features.

Ontario describes the dataset as non-overlapping 1 km by 1 km tiles grouped into downloadable packages.

Official catalogue:
https://data.ontario.ca/dataset/ontario-digital-surface-model-lidar-derived

Official GeoHub page:
https://geohub.lio.gov.on.ca/maps/mnrf::ontario-digital-surface-model-lidar-derived/about

Official DSM tile index:
https://www.publicdocs.mnr.gov.on.ca/mirb/OntarioDSM_LidarDerived_TileIndex.zip

Both supported datasets are published under the **Open Government Licence – Ontario**.

Users should review and comply with the current Ontario licence terms when using, redistributing, or creating products derived from the downloaded data.

## Attribution and independence

Ontario Elevation Manager is an independent QGIS plugin.

It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or maintained by the Government of Ontario, Geospatial Ontario, the Ontario Ministry of Natural Resources, Esri, OSGeo, or the QGIS project.

The plugin does not claim ownership of the Ontario datasets. It automates access to publicly available source data and services.

## Dataset characteristics

Both products compile multiple LiDAR acquisition projects. Specifications may vary between projects, including:

- acquisition date;
- resolution;
- coordinate reference system;
- vertical datum;
- sensor;
- accuracy;
- raster packaging/format.

Users remain responsible for reviewing source metadata and determining whether a dataset is suitable for a specific engineering, regulatory, scientific, or other use.
